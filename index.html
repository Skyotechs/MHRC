Why agencies choose MHRC

Paper logs create risks and gaps; by the time a supervisor or decision maker sees a paper log, the problem has impacted operations and delayed corrective actions. MHRC eliminates this. Deficiencies can be seen, tracked and resolved in real time.

Nothing falls through

Digital checklists guide each crew through their check. Once an item is flagged as missing, supervisory personnel are automatically and immediately informed. Supervisors must resolve the deficiency with their notes included in the rig check, providing full acountability.

Real-time alerts

A real time dashboard of trucks and inventory. Supervisors are alerted of and can see deficiencies immediately after it's noted; they also must resolve deficiencies before field staff can submit their final checks. Expiring inventory/certifications can get custom alerts for their expirations.

Fleet visibility

Track maintenance, inspections, registrations, and insurance due dates for vehicles with more custom alerts.

Inventory control

Monitor stock across multiple locations. Export inventory, personnel and rig checks.
